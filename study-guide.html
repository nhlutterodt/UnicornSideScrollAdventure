Multiplication Study Guide

Build confidence with multiplication facts, place value and multi-step reasoning.

Break numbers into smaller parts

For 23 × 4, split 23 into 20 and 3.

20 × 4 = 80 and 3 × 4 = 12. Add 80 + 12 = 92.

Multiplying by tens

Think of 6 × 40 as six groups of four tens. That's 24 tens, or 240.

Two-step story problems

Four classrooms each have six tables, and every table seats three students.

First find the tables: 4 × 6 = 24. Then find the seats: 24 × 3 = 72.
